Personal data processed

When the application starts it sends one verification request to the license server. The request carries the data below, which is stored on the server:

DataHow it is obtainedWhy it is processed
Device fingerprint (KDV-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) A SHA-256 digest of your Windows machine GUID (MachineGuid) and network card address (MAC). The raw hardware identifiers are never sent; only the digest is. Binding the license to the right device; preventing the 15-day trial from being restarted on the same machine or the same network
IP address Arrives with the verification request Security; detecting many devices behind one IP (marked “shared IP” in the admin panel) and granting the free trial per network (IP) rather than per device
Application version With the verification request Update information and technical support
Operating system information With the verification request Technical support
First and last seen timestamps Server record License term tracking
License status, term and seller notes Sales and authorization actions License and sales tracking

What stays on your computer

Your invoices, ledger records, control tables and every generated report are processed and stored only on your own computer. They are never sent to the server. There is no telemetry, usage analytics or screen recording in the application.

Collection method and legal basis

The technical data above is collected by the automatic HTTPS request the application makes at startup. The legal basis is the performance of the license contract and our legitimate interest (preventing license abuse).

Transfer abroad (Article 9, Law No. 6698)

The data is stored on the infrastructure of Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers & KV), our hosting provider. Cloudflare servers sit outside Türkiye, in various regions of the world, so the device fingerprint, IP address and technical records are transferred abroad.

RecipientCountryData transferredPurpose
Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers, KV) United States and global edge locations Device fingerprint, IP address, operating system, application version Licence verification and record keeping
GitHub, Inc. United States IP address (only during the update check) Checking whether a new version exists
Google LLC (Gmail) United States Your e-mail address and the contents of the correspondence Only if you send us an e-mail

The storage location cannot be chosen, so the transfer is inseparable from the service. For that reason the application asks for your explicit consent to the transfer abroad on first launch; without consent it does not open. Your consent is stored, with its date and text version, on your own computer in %USERPROFILE%\.kdv_kontrol\kvkk_onay.json. You may withdraw it at any time by writing to ardaekiz72@gmail.com.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts; fonts and icons are served from our own domain, so no third-party CDN request is made while you visit. The application contains no component that collects usage data.

Retention period

  • Licence record: while the licence is valid and for 2 years afterwards.
  • Server operation logs: at most the last 1,000 records; older records are deleted automatically.
  • E-mail correspondence: 2 years after the correspondence ends.
  • Data on your own computer: until you delete it.

To have your record deleted, write to ardaekiz72@gmail.com with your device fingerprint (shown in the application); the record is then deleted. Note that once the licence record is gone the application can no longer verify itself and locks.

Data breach notification

If a security breach affecting your personal data is detected:

  • The breach is notified to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board within 72 hours of our becoming aware of it.
  • Affected people are informed directly (by e-mail) as soon as reasonably possible.
  • The notification states the nature of the breach, the categories of data affected, the possible consequences and the measures taken.

Your rights (Article 11, Turkish Data Protection Law No. 6698)

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information if it has been processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly
  • Know the third parties to whom data is transferred, in Turkey or abroad
  • Request correction of incomplete or incorrect processing
  • Request deletion or destruction of the data
  • Request that corrections and deletions be notified to the third parties involved
  • Object to a result produced solely by automated analysis that works against you
  • Claim compensation if unlawful processing causes you damage

You can send these requests to the e-mail address or phone number above.
