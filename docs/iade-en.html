Returns and withdrawal.

If the licence does not suit you, you can withdraw within 14 days — without giving a reason.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

14-day right of withdrawal

Within 14 days of the licence being activated on your device you may withdraw from the contract without giving any reason and without paying a penalty. Having used the software during this period does not take away your right of withdrawal.

How to apply

  1. Write to ardaekiz72@gmail.com or call +90 546 208 38 54.
  2. Include the device fingerprint shown in the application (KDV-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX); it is used only to match your request to the right device.
  3. Once your request is received you get a confirmation e-mail and the licence is deactivated from your device.

Refund

The amount you paid is refunded within 14 days of your withdrawal notice, to the instrument you paid with (the bank account for transfers/EFT). No handling fee is deducted. A refund receipt is sent by e-mail.

What happens when the licence is deactivated

The application works by verifying its licence with the server. When the licence is deactivated the program locks, but the invoice, ledger and report files on your computer stay exactly as they are; nothing is deleted. What data exists and where it is kept is explained on the privacy page.

If the product is faulty

If you find a defect in the software you may use the statutory remedies under Law No. 6502 (free repair, price reduction or withdrawal from the contract) in addition to your right of withdrawal. Writing to ardaekiz72@gmail.com is enough.
