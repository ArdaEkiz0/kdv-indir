FAQ

Is the demo limited?

No. You get every feature and unlimited files for 15 days; the app only asks for a license once the period ends.

Do my invoices leave my computer?

No. All comparison happens locally. Apart from license verification, the app never sends any data anywhere.

Will my Excel export from the accounting software work?

Yes. Column matching is automatic for Luca, Logo and standard accounting outputs; for an unusual template you map it once and it is remembered for later periods.

Can I run several periods at once?

Yes. Feed it a yearly trial balance or a multi-period sheet; results can also be filtered and reported per period.

Can the license move to another computer?

A key is bound to one machine; after a format or a new PC, email us and we transfer it.
