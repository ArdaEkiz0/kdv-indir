Two folders. One check.

Load. Choose your invoice folder and your ledger folder (you can also pick individual files; subfolders are included). The program matches records by document number, then compares amount and VAT.

Review. Every row is listed with its own status: matched, amount difference, VKN difference, not in ledger, not in invoices, duplicate. Double-click a row to open the full document detail in a separate window.

Save. Get your Excel report, PDF report, Ba/Bs form and accountant package in one click. Each run is compared against the previous one; newly missing and resolved documents are flagged in the summary strip.

Frequently asked

Do my records leave my computer?

No. The program runs locally; your invoices and reports never leave your computer.

Which files can I load?

Invoices: PDF, Excel (.xlsx, .xlsm, .xls), XML and single-file .zip archives. Ledger (KDV control list): PDF and Excel. Subfolders are included.

Do I need to install it?

Optional. The installer does not ask for administrator rights; you can also download the portable version and run it directly.

What happens when the licence period ends?

The program locks; your work stays safe. The licence is granted to this device from the server; there is no key entry on any screen. Get in touch for the full version.

Do I need to enter a licence key?

No. The licence is granted to this device from the server; there is no key entry.

Is there a trial?

Yes. The trial starts when the program first connects to the licence server and lasts 15 days; no credit card required. The trial runs for 15 days per network (IP), not per device: a second computer on the same office/network does not get a fresh 15 days, it inherits the network’s remaining time. A new installation that has not yet reached the server runs for at most 3 days without internet; you need to connect once for the trial to start.
