Three files. One check.

Load. Choose your sales invoices and e-Invoice records; add the previous period report for a period-over-period comparison. The program matches records by invoice number, date, amount and VAT.

Review. The result screen shows the match status next to every row: matched, amount mismatch, VAT mismatch, duplicate, not in e-Invoice, extra in e-Invoice. You can correct the VAT rate and amount right in the table.

Save. Export the report as Excel, PDF or a printable output and fill in your VAT return from this table. Next period, new records are flagged automatically against the previous period report.

Invoices are matched

Sales invoice rows are matched to e-Invoice records by number and amount.

Differences are flagged

Rows whose amount or VAT does not match are coloured together with the field that differs.

Into the record

Every row gets its result; you carry the results straight into the report.
