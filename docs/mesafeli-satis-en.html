Distance sales agreement.

Pre-contractual information and agreement text under the Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation. The Turkish text is the governing version.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Parties

SELLER: Arda Mehmet Ekiz (independent software developer).
E-mail: ardaekiz72@gmail.com · Phone: +90 546 208 38 54

BUYER: the person requesting the purchase; name, e-mail and invoicing details are taken at the time of the order.

2. Subject

This agreement governs the sale and delivery of the KDV Cross-Check desktop software licence purchased electronically from the SELLER, and sets out the rights and obligations of the parties. The product is a desktop software licence for Windows 10 and 11; there is no physical delivery.

Trial period: Before purchasing, the BUYER may try the software free of charge. The trial lasts 15 days and starts the first time the program connects to the licence server; all features are open during that period. The trial is granted per internet connection (network), not per device: a second computer on the same network does not get a fresh 15 days, it inherits the remaining time of that network. A new installation that has not yet reached the server runs for at most 3 days without an internet connection; the trial can only start once the program has connected to the server. The trial is free, creates no payment obligation, and once it ends the software cannot be used without purchasing a licence.

3. Price and payment

No price list is published on this site. The licence fee and payment method (bank transfer, EFT or similar) are stated in writing to the BUYER before the order, in response to the purchase request; the stated amount and payment details form an integral part of this agreement. The BUYER may request a receipt or invoice after paying.

4. Delivery

After payment, the SELLER activates the licence on the BUYER's device from the server. There is no licence key to type in; the application verifies the licence at startup. The BUYER is notified once activation is complete.

5. Right of withdrawal

The BUYER may withdraw from the contract within 14 days, without giving any reason and without paying a penalty. The withdrawal period starts on the day the licence is activated on the device. Notice of withdrawal can be sent to ardaekiz72@gmail.com or made by phone. On withdrawal the licence is deactivated from the device and the fee is refunded to the BUYER's payment instrument within 14 days of the notice. Details are on the Returns and withdrawal page.

6. General provisions

By confirming the pre-contractual information electronically, the BUYER accepts the formation of the distance contract. Rights arising from Law No. 6502 in case of defective goods are reserved. Within the monetary limits announced by the Ministry of Trade, the parties may apply to the Consumer Arbitration Committees and Consumer Courts at the BUYER's place of residence.

7. Entry into force

This agreement enters into force when the BUYER submits the purchase request and pays the fee. The text is kept by the parties electronically.
